Settings

Timer display

Auto follows the length of the sit: the ring for one minute, the hourglass for three, the incense for five and beyond. Incense needs a sit of five minutes or more; shorter sits show the ring.

The bell

Sound it once to check the volume before you sit. On phones that can, the bell is also felt as a short vibration, so a silenced phone still marks the end.
When on, a single touch during the sit asks for the stick: a bow, then a sharp strike to bring you back. When off, a touch simply shows pause and end.

The vows

Spoken at the close of each sitting. Leave a line empty to omit it. Changes are kept as you type.

Mark a past sitting

For a sit lost to a reset, or done away from the app.

Backup

Your record and vows live on this device. Copy a backup, or paste one to restore — useful before clearing Safari data or moving to a new phone.
When on, your sittings are not added to the record. For trying things out.
